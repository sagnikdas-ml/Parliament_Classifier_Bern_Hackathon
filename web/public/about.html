THE IDEA

News, checked against the record.

ParlaRadar helps journalists trace political claims to Swiss parliamentary records.

01

Read sentence by sentence

Each sentence is assessed in context. Hover, focus or tap to see its result.

02

Follow the evidence

Checks search OpenParlData records stored in DuckDB. Sources, dates and speakers appear when the archive provides them.

03

Keep uncertainty visible

Missing evidence is not proof a claim is false. Suggested corrections need supporting sources and editorial review.

04

Explore topics

Research APS topics or define a custom topic, then classify parliamentary affairs across the indexed archive.
